Small websites get better when they stay easy to change.

## A few rules

- Keep content close to the code that renders it.
- Prefer static generation until you actually need runtime behavior.
- Make deployment boring and predictable.
- Choose a design language that can survive many posts.

## Why this matters

The best personal sites are maintained over time, not launched once and forgotten. A simple workflow makes that possible.
